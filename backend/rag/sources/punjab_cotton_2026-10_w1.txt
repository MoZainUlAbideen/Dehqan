COTTON PEST MANAGEMENT ADVISORY: 
Sucking Insect Pests (Whitefly, Jassid & Thrips) 
 
1. Regular pest scouting twice in a week. 
2. Avoid excessive use of nitrogenous fertilizers  
3. Supply light irrigation and reduce interval between irrigations. 
4. Apply yellow sticky traps @ 15 -20 per acre and repeat/ replace it at fortnight 
interval. 
5. Use Chrysoperla carnea cards @20 cards/acre. 
6. Spray recommended insecticide s at ETL with proper dose, time  and spray 
equipment and with proper nozzle i.e. hollow cone nozzle. 
7. Ensure proper coverage of spray with optimum swath width and use only power 
sprayer or tractor mounted boom sprayer, avoid spray with battery operated -
knapsacks sprayer. 
8. Avoid spraying the insecticide from the same group of insecticide. 
9. Preferably use safe chemistry insecticides as these are comparatively safe for 
beneficial fauna. 
10. Spray pesticides preferably in the evening time.

Cotton Leaf Curl Virus 
 
 Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV) is transmitted rapidly by whitefly. Therefore, its 
management should include the judicious use of recommended insecticides along 
with biological control measures. 
 Remove and destroy weeds growing in orchards, along water channels (Khals), 
field bunds and othe r nearby areas, as these may serve as alternate hosts for 
whitefly and virus infection. 
 For improved crop growth and development, apply a foliar spray of 250 g Zinc 
Sulphate (33%), 250 g Borax, and 300 g Magnesium Sulphate dissolved in 100 
litres of water.
